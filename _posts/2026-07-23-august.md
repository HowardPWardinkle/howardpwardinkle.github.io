## Idea

This is the third in a three-card series about the American space program from the 1960s. [Last month's](2026/06/29/july.html) card covered the Gemini program and [June's](/2026/05/29/june.html) the Mercury program. This one covers the Apollo program, in particular Apollo 11's moon landing. This idea was the first one for the series. I thought of a mouse landing on the moon, excited that he had found it was made of cheese. I thought of other space-related ideas such as mission control, etc., but came up with the series of three for the three programs. When I started working on this one, I decided to make it more "realistic" by having the mouse land on the moon and be disappointed it was *not* made of cheese.

> By the way, July 20 of this year was the 57th anniversary of the Apollo 11 moon landing.

While researching this card, I stumbled upon this toy. I remember having it as a kid and how hard it was to get his hands into the gloves.

![GI Joe Space Capsule](/images/2608/gi-joe.png){: width="{{ site.imageWidthTwoThird }}" }
